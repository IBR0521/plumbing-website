About

We’d rather you never need the emergency line.

That’s the thinking behind the tag. Most flood damage happens in the minutes someone spends hunting for the valve.

The short version

Ridgeback is a local plumbing company that does two things most: emergency repairs at any hour, and replacing worn-out pipe in older houses. The two are connected. A lot of the 2 a.m. calls come from galvanized lines that finally gave up.

On every job we find the main shutoff, check that it turns, and hang a numbered yellow tag on it. That number points to a record of your house: what the pipes are made of, what we fixed, and what’s worth watching. The next time you call, read us the number and we start with the facts.
